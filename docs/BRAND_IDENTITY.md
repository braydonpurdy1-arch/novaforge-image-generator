# NovaForge Studios Brand Identity

## Canonical public contact

NovaForge Studios uses **Nova@NovaForgeStudios.com** as the public-facing inbox and contact identity.

Canonical email address: `nova@novaforgestudios.com`

The display casing may be shown as `Nova@NovaForgeStudios.com`, but systems should store and compare the canonical lowercase address.

## Identity model

- **Organization:** NovaForge Studios
- **Domain:** `novaforgestudios.com`
- **Main public inbox:** `nova@novaforgestudios.com`
- **Public identity:** Nova
- **Nova's role:** central NovaForge AI / main assistant identity and public front door
- **Founder / CEO work email:** `braydon@novaforgestudios.com`

## Usage rules

1. Public contact surfaces should default to `nova@novaforgestudios.com`.
2. `braydon@novaforgestudios.com` is the founder/CEO's individual work identity and should not replace the general public inbox.
3. Product UI, documentation, websites, CRM records, support surfaces and future business systems should use the Nova public inbox unless a named human contact is specifically required.
4. Future role addresses such as `support@`, `billing@`, `security@`, `careers@` or `legal@` may be added without changing the primary public identity.
5. Creative providers and external apps must not gain vehicle-critical access merely because they use the NovaForge brand identity.

## Source of truth

The machine-readable companion file is `config/brand-identity.json`. Changes to the canonical public identity should update both files through reviewed repository changes.
